What is a DU?

One d0d Unit (DU)-hour is one worker CPU running for one hour, with its memory, disk and network included. It's the same idea as a BigQuery slot or a Databricks DBU. What's different is what we don't bill:

  • Per second, no minimum. A 3-second question is billed for 3 seconds.
  • $0 while idle. Workers shut down when there's no work.
  • $0 for bad plans. They're rejected in microseconds, before any data is read.
  • $0 for cached answers and dashboard refreshes of unchanged data.
  • $0 for planning. The model that plans your question runs inside dOd, so there are no per-token AI charges.

A rule of thumb: a simple filter or count over 1 billion records takes about 2 DU-minutes, roughly a third of a cent. Joins and percentiles use more.
