FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do my agents (or I) need to know SQL?

No. Agents and people ask in plain English. dOd's embedded map-reduce model builder turns that into an exact plan — no translation to SQL, no guessing — and runs it over all your data, even terabytes. dOd shows how it worked out every answer in a sentence, so you can check it.

What can I connect?

Google Sheets, PostgreSQL, BigQuery, Amazon S3, Google Cloud Storage, Apache Iceberg, and CSV or Parquet file uploads. More sources are coming — tell us what you use.

Is my data safe?

dOd reads your data where it lives instead of copying your database, and connection details are encrypted in your workspace. With Google Sheets, dOd can open only the spreadsheets you pick. Use a read-only login for databases.

Can my team use it?

Yes. Invite editors who ask questions, and give everyone else a dashboard to view — dashboard viewers are unlimited on every paid plan.

What does it cost?

The demo is free with no card. Connecting your own data starts at $49 a month, or $490 a year. Change plans or cancel any time from your billing page. There are no per-token AI charges — you pay for infrastructure only.
