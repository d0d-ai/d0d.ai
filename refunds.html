Refund Policy

Last updated October 6, 2026

Try dOd free on your own data before you pay — no card needed. If a paid plan still isn't right for you, here's how refunds work.

14-day money-back guarantee

If you're not happy, ask for a refund within 14 days of your first payment on a plan, monthly or yearly, and we'll refund it in full. No questions asked.

Renewals

Subscriptions renew automatically. If a renewal charge surprised you, ask within 14 days of the renewal and we'll refund it, provided the workspace hasn't been used since the renewal. Otherwise, cancel any time from your billing page: you won't be charged again, and you keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

Yearly plans

After the first 14 days, cancelling a yearly plan stops the next renewal; the current year is not refunded pro rata, except where the law requires it or we discontinue your plan.

How to ask

Email a@d0d.ai from your account's email address, or contact Paddle, our Merchant of Record, through paddle.net. Refunds go back to the original payment method and are processed by Paddle, usually within 5–10 business days.

Your legal rights

This policy doesn't affect any rights you have under consumer law where you live.
