Privacy Policy

Last updated October 7, 2026

This policy explains what personal information d0d.ai ("dOd", "we", "us") collects when you use d0d.ai and workspace.d0d.ai (the "Service"), how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account information: your name, email address and sign-in details, and the people you invite to your workspace.
  • Your connected data: the data sources you connect and the connection details for them (stored encrypted), and the questions, dashboards and charts you create. We read your data where it lives to answer your questions; we do not copy your databases.
  • Billing information: payments are handled by Paddle, our Merchant of Record. Paddle collects your payment details; we receive your plan, billing country and payment status, never your full card number.
  • Usage and device information: logs of requests to the Service, such as IP address, browser type and times, used to run, secure and fix the Service.
  • Messages: what you send us by email or support.

Questions typed into the live demo on d0d.ai are sent to our demo workspace and may be logged to improve the demo. Please don't type personal information there.

How we use it

We use personal information to provide and secure the Service, answer your questions, process payments, provide support, send you service and billing messages, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use your data to train AI models.

AI processing

To plan how to answer a question, dOd sends the question and a description of your data's structure (such as table and column names) to a language model provider. The rows of your tables are not sent to the language model; answers are computed by dOd's own engine.

Who we share it with

We share personal information only with service providers who help us run the Service, under agreements that limit their use of it: cloud hosting, language model providers (as described above), Paddle for payments, and email delivery. We may also disclose information when the law requires it, or as part of a merger or sale of our business, in which case this policy continues to apply.

Cookies

The Service uses cookies needed to keep you signed in. This website uses Google Analytics, which sets cookies to count visits and see which pages and links are used, including which campaign or ad brought you here; Google receives your IP address and browser details to do this. We don't use advertising cookies. The website also loads fonts from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address when it serves them. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active. When you delete your workspace or account, we delete your data from the Service within 30 days, except records we must keep by law, such as billing records.

Security

Connection details are encrypted, traffic is sent over HTTPS, and access to production systems is limited. No system is perfectly secure; tell us at a@d0d.ai if you find a problem.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, including under the GDPR and CCPA, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, and to object to or restrict how we use it. Email a@d0d.ai and we will respond within 30 days. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

International transfers

We and our providers may process information in countries other than yours. Where required, we use safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

Children

The Service is for businesses and is not directed at children under 16.

Changes

We will post updates here and tell you by email of material changes.
